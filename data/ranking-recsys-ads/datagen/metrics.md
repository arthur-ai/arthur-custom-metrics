Top-K Ranking Quality,Computes NDCG@K MAP@K MRR@K Precision@K Recall@K and Hit Rate@K per time bucket from impression-level logs (one row per request and position) using clicked or relevance_grade as the relevance signal. Measures whether engaged items are ranked near the top of each slate.
Top-K Ranking Quality (Slate Arrays),Same ranking metrics for datasets that log one row per request with an ordered array of recommended items and an array of engaged items. Validated to match the impression-level version on this dataset.
Calibration Profile,Calibration ratio (sum of predicted click probability over observed clicks) expected calibration error and maximum calibration error per time bucket. Detects the ranker_v2 score inflation built into this dataset.
Reliability Diagram Bins,Mean predicted probability observed click rate calibration gap and volume share for each equal-width score bin (dimension score_bin). Powers a reliability diagram chart.
Catalog Coverage and Exposure,Share of the 2000-item catalog served per day share of impressions going to the top-N items Gini coefficient of item exposure and effective catalog size. Detects the popularity-bias shift built into this dataset.
Slate Diversity,Average slate size distinct categories per slate intra-list diversity (share of item pairs in different categories) normalized category entropy and the share of single-category slates.
